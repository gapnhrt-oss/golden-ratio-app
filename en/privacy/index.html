🔒 Privacy policy

How GOLDEN RATIO collects, uses and protects your data, and your rights over it.

Last updated: 2026-11-04

Introduction

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the GOLDEN RATIO website and app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how you can control it.

Data we collect

• Account data: name, email, WhatsApp number and password (stored hashed — we cannot see it).

• Subscription data: plan, start and end dates, and the payment receipt you upload.

• Usage data: pages you visit, signals you open, and a device identifier (used to prevent account sharing).

• Messages you send us through the in-site chat.

• Your alert settings and push-notification subscription on your device.

• Technical error reports (browser type and page) so we can fix problems.

How we use it

• To run your account and activate your subscription.

• To deliver signals and alerts on the site, WhatsApp and push notifications — according to your choices.

• To protect accounts from sharing and unauthorized use.

• To improve the service and fix errors.

We do not use your data for advertising and we never sell it.

Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary with providers that run the service:

• Supabase: database and sign-in.

• GitHub Pages: website hosting.

• Our WhatsApp delivery provider: only your number and the alert text, if you enable WhatsApp alerts.

• Telegram and X: we publish public content only, never personal data.

• Google Fonts and CDNs: to load fonts and files; they may see your IP address.

• Google Translate: if you choose English, the site's public interface text is sent for translation (not your personal data).

Storage on your device

We use your browser's local storage (localStorage) to keep you signed in and to remember your language, chart and alert settings. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Payment receipts and subscription records are kept as long as accounting requires. Visit and error logs are deleted or aggregated anonymously after a period.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correct it, delete your account entirely, or stop alerts at any time. WhatsApp alerts can be stopped from “My plan” or by replying “إيقاف”. For any request, contact us on WhatsApp.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and every table is protected by access rules that prevent any subscriber from seeing another's data. Only the administrator can see subscriber data, for the purpose of operating the service.

Age

The service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

We may update this policy and will show the date of the latest update at the top. If a change is significant, we will notify you on the site.
